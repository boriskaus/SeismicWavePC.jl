Output

Seismograms

simulate returns Seismograms: result.t (times), result.v (velocity [m/s]), result.u (displacement [m]), result.stress [Pa] and result.strain, each as [it, component, station], with z positive up; result["name"] gives the velocities of one station. write_sac(result, dir) writes SAC files as OpenSWPC does.

Seismic Unix. write_su(result, dir; quantities) writes SU files as SOFI does: one file per quantity and component (swpc.psv.vx.su, …), one trace per station, with the station (and source) positions, offset, sample interval and delay in the trace headers. Quantities: :v, :u, :stress, :strain, :p (pressure, minus the mean normal stress) and :div, :curl. read_su reads SU files (of either byte order), e.g. a recorded source wavelet: su = read_su("wavelet.su"); stf = SampledSTF(su.t, su.data[:, 1]).

Divergence and curl. simulate(...; derivatives = true) also returns the divergence and the curl of the velocity at every station (result.div[it, station], result.curl[it, component, station] with components x, y, z [1/s]): P and S energy, rotational motion. They are 4th-order differences of the velocity at auxiliary stations ±dh, ±2dh around each station (dh the grid spacing by default); at stations on the surface the vertical derivatives are one-sided from below. For an explosion in a homogeneous medium the curl is zero to round-off and the divergence the rate of the volumetric strain (3 %); at a free surface they satisfy the free-surface conditions to 1–3 %.

Wavefield and movies

  • result.wavefield: the velocity field at the end, as a CartData (collected on one process; wavefield = false leaves it out).
  • snapshot_every = 100: writes the velocity field to snapshot_dir as a ParaView movie (wavefield.pvd).
  • callback = (wavefield, t) -> …: called every snapshot_every steps with the wavefield as a CartData (field V), instead of writing files, e.g. to plot the waves while the simulation runs (see Getting started and the movies in Examples).
  • snapshots = Snapshots(xy=-5.0, xz=0.0, fs=true, quantities=(:v, :u, :ps)): planes (horizontal, vertical, free surface, ocean bottom) in OpenSWPC's netCDF format, including peak ground velocity maps (needs using NCDatasets). On several MPI processes, process 0 collects every plane and writes the files. With pieces = true every process writes the points of its own subdomain to files of its own (swpc.3d.fs.v.0000.nc, …), without communication, so that no process holds a whole plane: for runs on many processes or with large planes.
  • s = read_snapshots("snapshots/swpc.3d.fs.v.nc") reads the snapshots of a plane, whether in one file or in pieces: s.axes, s.t, s["Vx", k] (snapshot k), s["max-H"] (a field without time).
