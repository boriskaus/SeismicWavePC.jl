SeismicWavePC.jl

Seismic wave propagation and full-waveform inversion (FWI) in 2D and 3D visco-elastic media, in pure Julia, on CPUs, GPUs and MPI clusters.

SeismicWavePC.jl simulates seismic waves through any model built with GeophysicalModelGenerator.jl (GMG) and computes exact misfit gradients for full-waveform inversion. It started as a Julia port of the finite-difference code OpenSWPC (Maeda et al., 2017), still reproduces its seismograms to single-precision round-off (Validation), and has since grown well beyond it.

Features

  • Physics: P-SV and SH (2D) and 3D visco-elastic waves on a staggered grid (4th order in space, 2nd order in time); constant Q (generalized Zener body); free surfaces and topography; oceans; PML, multiaxial PML or Cerjan absorbing boundaries.
  • Topography on curvilinear grids: a terrain-following mesh with the free surface exactly on the topography and grid lines along interfaces, with provably stable summation-by-parts (SBP) finite differences and simultaneous approximation terms; 2D and 3D, attenuation, VTI, PML, gradients and Born modelling, GPUs and MPI (Topography on a curvilinear grid).
  • Sources and receivers: moment tensors, double couples, forces and plane waves, in Cartesian or geographic coordinates; velocity, displacement, stress and strain seismograms; reciprocal Green's functions.
  • Hardware: Julia threads on CPUs (faster than OpenSWPC per core and in total), GPUs from NVIDIA, AMD and Apple, and MPI for several GPUs or nodes.
  • Models: any GMG CartData: GMG's geology tools and geographic data, thermal models (MagmaThermoKinematics.jl) and phase diagrams (MAGEMin), random media, OpenSWPC's model files.
  • Full-waveform inversion (FWI): exact adjoint-state gradients of least-squares, travel-time or custom misfits with respect to the model (rho, vp, vs, Qp, Qs) and the sources, for several shots, at the cost of about 5 simulations, on CPUs, GPUs and MPI (Misfit gradients).
  • Deterministic and Bayesian FWI: Optim.jl, Turing.jl (Hamiltonian Monte Carlo with any of its AD backends), AdvancedHMC.jl, DynamicHMC.jl and other LogDensityProblems.jl samplers (Inversion and sampling packages).
  • Output: seismograms in memory, the wavefield as CartData, ParaView movies, or your own plots while the simulation runs; SAC and netCDF files as in OpenSWPC (Coming from OpenSWPC).

Reference

Maeda, T., Takemura, S., and Furumura, T. (2017). OpenSWPC: an open-source integrated parallel simulation code for modeling seismic wave propagation in 3D heterogeneous viscoelastic media. Earth, Planets and Space, 69, 102. doi:10.1186/s40623-017-0687-2

The curvilinear solver (topography and interfaces on a terrain-following grid) uses summation-by-parts operators from SummationByPartsOperators.jl:

Ranocha, H. (2021). SummationByPartsOperators.jl: A Julia library of provably stable semidiscretization techniques with mimetic properties. Journal of Open Source Software, 6(64), 3454. doi:10.21105/joss.03454

Mattsson, K., Niemelä, O., and Winters, A. R. (2027). Boundary-optimized closures for diagonal-norm upwind SBP operators. Journal of Computational Physics, 568, 115399. doi:10.1016/j.jcp.2026.115399

Mattsson, K. (2017). Diagonal-norm upwind SBP operators. Journal of Computational Physics, 335, 283–310.
